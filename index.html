01 / About

Experience that connects public administration with delivery.

Haris Bin Maqsood served at Pakistan’s Employees’ Old-Age Benefits Institution (EOBI) from 1995 until September 2026, retiring as Deputy Director General (BS-20) and Head of Information Technology.

His background includes nationwide IT operations, institutional reporting, technology procurement and government-side tender processes from planning and evaluation through approvals and awards. He holds an MSc in Statistics and an MBA, with over two decades of university teaching experience.
